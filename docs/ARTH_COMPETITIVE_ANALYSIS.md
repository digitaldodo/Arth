**Method:** public product/help/pricing pages and official Income Tax Department materials. Feature descriptions below are vendor-published claims, not independent product testing. Availability, pricing and details can change; pages should be rechecked before launch. Public prices were not consistently exposed by the reviewed pages, so this document does not infer them.

## Product implications for Arth

Competitors already advertise password-protected statement imports, categorized rows, review queues and some duplicate checks. Those are table stakes. TallyPrime documents format constraints and reconciliation workflows; consumer products describe user review and account matching. Tax products already offer regime calculators and Form 16 workflows. Arth should therefore differentiate through measurable **cross-account reconciliation**, **source-to-ledger-to-tax provenance**, and **explainable, year-versioned tax comparisons** rather than claims of universal extraction or AI accuracy.

## Evidence and claims policy

- The observations are based on public pages as accessed on 2026-11-09; they are not hands-on acceptance testing.
- No competitor pricing is asserted because the pages reviewed did not consistently expose comparable prices.
- No claim that Arth outperforms a competitor is made. Any future comparative claim needs a reproducible benchmark and dated source.
- Product support, privacy practices and prices may change; verify before making public positioning claims.
